The Atelier

A small house, designing for those who prefer not to be found.

My Tour EC began in Quito in 1998 as a single travel desk serving a handful of diplomatic families. Three decades on, the principle hasn't changed — we craft private journeys for a small number of travelers each year, often returning the same guests across generations.

We work with one captain in the Galápagos. One chef in the Old Town. One guide who has tracked jaguars in Yasuní for twenty-two years. Specificity is luxury. Trust is currency.

We do not publish itineraries. Each journey is drawn by hand, in consultation, often over months. Our travelers describe what they imagine. We compose the rest.
